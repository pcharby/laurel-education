## What this addendum covers (per Google's published terms)

- **Subprocessors** (Section 11): Google may engage third-party
  subprocessors to process Customer Data; Laurel Education has the right
  to object to new subprocessors Google adds in the future.
- **Security commitments** (Section 7): Documented technical,
  organizational, and physical security measures; ISO 27001 and SOC 2/3
  certifications for audited services; data incident notification
  requirements.
- **International data transfers** (Section 10): Subject to Google's
  data location commitments — relevant here because Firestore/Functions
  are pinned to Canadian regions, per the app's own configuration.
